---
description: Draft polite follow-ups for unpaid invoices or unanswered quotes.
---
1. Find what is overdue: from the invoice summary in `reports/`, a sheet the owner named,
   or by asking. Never invent amounts or dates.
2. For each client, check the last email thread with `gmail__search_emails` so the follow-up
   does not repeat a message sent in the last 3 days.
3. Draft one email per client with `gmail__create_draft`: invoice number, amount, due date,
   a friendly tone, the owner's sign-off, in the client's language.
4. VIP contacts from OWNER.md: draft only, and flag them in the reply.
5. Reply with the list of drafts (client, amount, days overdue). Never send them yourself.
